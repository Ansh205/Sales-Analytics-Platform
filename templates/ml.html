Why Baseline R² is Negative (-0.0017)
The mean baseline predicts the training set target mean. When evaluated on the held-out test set, slight differences between train and test means cause the residual sum of squares to exceed the total sum of squares ($R^2 = 1 - SS_{\text{res}} / SS_{\text{tot}} < 0$). This reflects true sampling variation and is not hidden.
Why Tree Models Outperformed Linear Regression (63% MAE Reduction)
Net profit is fundamentally multiplicative: $\text{Profit} \approx \text{Sales} \times (1 - \text{Discount}) - \text{COGS}$. Standard linear regression cannot capture this non-linear interaction without explicit product terms. Tree models split the continuous feature space to identify steep margin collapse points.
